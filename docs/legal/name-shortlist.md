# New name for "ProjectOS": shortlist and pre-screen

Prepared 2026-10-29 for RBT Systems Limited (UK).

> **CAVEAT: this is a pre-screen, not a clearance search.** It is a knockout pass using public
> registers and the open web. It is not legal advice and it is not a trade mark clearance
> opinion. It does not cover unregistered (common-law / passing-off) rights in full, pending
> applications not yet published, or every national register. It cannot judge how an examiner or
> court would weigh similarity. Before filing or launching, have a UK trade mark attorney run a
> full clearance search on the chosen name (UK, EU, US and any target country).

## Why a new name is needed (confirmed)

- **PROJECTOS**, US SN 99201702, Infinite Snacks LLC (Delaware). Filed 2025-05-25, live. Class 42: "SaaS
  featuring software for project management, task tracking, and team collaboration…" (USPTO
  tmsearch record, read 2026-10-29).
- A live "ProjectOS" product at projectos.dev (as stated in the brief).

## Top 3: reasons and the next step for each

### 1. Ruvane
- **Why:** a truly coined word, so it is the strongest mark type. No RUVANE mark in classes 9/35/42
  at USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO or any TMview office. All five non-.com domains are available at standard
  prices. Short (two syllables), easy to spell, and no negative meaning found. "Vane" gives a
  subtle sense of direction. Works as an umbrella brand: Ruvane desktop, Ruvane Cloud, Ruvane
  companion app.
- **Watch:** a five-day-old hobby GitHub project, `ruvane-rs` (a Rust web framework), has no
  registration but is in software. Ruvane.com is parked at GoDaddy and has been held since 2005.
- **Next step:** (1) today, buy **ruvane.ai, ruvane.io, ruvane.app, ruvane.dev and ruvane.co.uk**
  (about $200 in total). (2) Commission a UK trade mark attorney's full clearance search for UK,
  EU and US. (3) If clear, **file at UK IPO in classes 9 and 42**, adding 35 if hosted or business
  services will be sold. The current online fee is £170 plus £50 per extra class; confirm before
  filing. (4) Within 6 months, extend to the EU and US (Madrid Protocol or direct) **claiming UK
  priority**. (5) Optionally, make an offer for ruvane.com through GoDaddy Domain Broker.

### 2. Steerhold
- **Why:** best fit for the product. "Steer" is direction, approved plans and owner control.
  "Hold" is spending caps and the emergency stop. Zero exact marks anywhere, the nearest marks are
  in unrelated goods (STEELHOLD, DEERHOLD), and no products or companies use the name. All
  non-.com domains are available. It is suggestive rather than descriptive, so it is registrable,
  though it is a little weaker than a coined word.
- **Watch:** **steerhold.com was registered on 2026-10-01** (Spaceship, parking page). If the owner
  did not register it, someone else is holding it.
- **Next step:** (1) today, buy **steerhold.ai, .io, .app, .dev and .co.uk**. (2) Check whether
  RBT/the owner registered steerhold.com. If not, make an offer through Spaceship or the RDAP
  registrant contact. (3) Get attorney clearance, then **file at UK IPO in classes 9 and 42** (+35),
  followed by EU and US within the 6-month priority window.

### 3. Zarvel (conditional)
- **Why:** coined, short, sounds good, "zar" = gold in Urdu/Persian/Hindi. All non-.com domains are
  available and the **.com looks buyable** (it redirects to the branded.org marketplace). It is the
  only top-3 name with a realistic route to the .com.
- **Watch:** it rhymes with **MARVEL**, which has many live class 9/35/42 registrations and enough
  fame for a dilution or opposition argument. This must be cleared first.
- **Next step:** (1) get an **attorney knockout opinion specifically on MARVEL**, for UK/EU/US
  confusion and dilution, before spending money. (2) If cleared, buy **zarvel.ai, .io, .app, .dev
  and .co.uk** and ask branded.org for the price of zarvel.com. (3) **File at UK IPO in classes 9
  and 42** (+35).

**Runner-up if a meaningful compound is preferred over Zarvel:** Truekeel (clean registers, but the
.ai and .com are held by an unknown party who may be planning to use the name). **Fairhelm is not
recommended** despite its good meaning: a Bangalore software company already trades as "Fairhelm
Systems", and FINHELM is pending in class 42 AI SaaS.

## Things to know before acting

1. **Coined names are being claimed fast, probably because AI name generators suggest the same
   ones.** Within the past year: dovrin.com (2026-06, now an AI product), quorvel.com (2026-04),
   truekeel.ai (2026-03), mastwise.com (2026-09-29), steerhold.com (2026-10-01), and the `ruvane-rs`
   GitHub org (2026-10-24). Once the owner picks a name, buy the domains **the same day** and file
   the UK application promptly. UK filing date sets priority.
2. RDAP "no record" does not guarantee a domain is available, because premium and reserved names
   exist. Every domain marked "avail" here was also confirmed by a registrar availability API on
   2026-10-29. Prices are a registrar's list prices and vary by registrar.
3. **Classes to file:** 9 (downloadable desktop software, CLI, service, mobile companion app) and
   42 (SaaS/hosted service, AI software services). Add 35 only for business-management or
   advertising services. Consider 38 if the companion app's messaging becomes a headline feature
   and 41 for training. Choose the specification wording with the attorney.
4. Because the company may move country later, a UK filing first, then the Madrid Protocol within
   6 months, keeps one priority date for all later countries.
